# Package Name Viewer – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Package Name Viewer ("the app") is published by Lunar. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

## Summary
- The app **does not collect, store on any server, or share** any personal or device data.
- The app **has no internet permission**. It cannot send anything off your phone.
- There are **no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts**.

## Information the app reads
To do its job, the app reads the list of apps installed on your device using Android's `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` permission. For each app it reads details provided by Android: app name, icon, package name, version, target and minimum SDK, install source, install and update dates, APK size, UID, requested permissions and declared components (activities, services, receivers, providers).

This information is shown **only to you, on your device**. It is not sent anywhere.

## Exporting
If you choose **Export**, the app creates a CSV or text file of your installed apps. The file is only shared or saved where **you** choose (for example a file location or another app you pick in the share sheet). The app does not upload it.

## Settings
Your theme choice (system, light or dark) is stored locally on your device.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
